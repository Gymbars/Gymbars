# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: people who want to start training at Gymbars (new members). Secondary: current members who check schedules and routines. The new-member path comes first: a visitor should understand the training quickly and know how to join or get in touch.

## Product Purpose
Gymbars is a training space built around calisthenics. The site presents the gym, its training programs (Rutinas de Calistenia), the schedule, the location and contact options, and lets a visitor start a membership conversation. Success means a visitor understands the training in one viewport and contacts the gym or visits.

## Positioning
Calisthenics is the priority. The gym has several bar sizes, a rope, parallel bars and many other calisthenics tools, and it also has some weights for strength training. Weights are a supporting part of the offer, not the center. A neighboring gym with machines and free weights cannot truthfully claim the bar-based, bodyweight focus.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive on a phone or desktop, often from social media or a search. Routines are published as individual pages with instructions and exercise lists. Schedule and contact details are edited by the gym in a Google Sheet and appear on the site after reload (cached about 5 minutes).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Content (schedule, routines, carousel, features, contact, social links) is managed in a Google Sheet through the Gymbars admin panel; the design must work with variable content and missing images.
- Delivered as a Google Apps Script web app; no build step.
- Spanish-language copy. Keep the existing Spanish labels and content from the sheet.
- Equipment facts (bar sizes, rope, parallels, tools, weights) are confirmed by the gym owner. Do not add numbers or claims beyond these.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Gymbars (keep).
- Existing brand colors are not binding: the owner is open to new colors.
- Existing photos from Google Drive are real gym photos and should be used.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real gym photos exist in the Google Drive folder the site already uses (carousel, feature cards, routine images).
- No official logo file was confirmed.
- Location, street address and the preferred contact channel are not confirmed yet. Do not invent them; keep the existing sheet-driven fields.

## Product Principles
- Calisthenics first: bars, rings, rope and parallels lead; weights support.
- Make joining easy: the next step is always clear.
- Real content only: no invented testimonials, prices, or statistics.
- Works with incomplete content: missing photos and fields must still look intentional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Spanish-speaking visitors on mobile are the main audience. Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 and visible keyboard focus are required.
